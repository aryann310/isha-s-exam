### Question 3
- **Section**: General English
- **Topic**: Active and Passive Voice
- **Difficulty**: Easy

**Question:**
Select the option that represents the given sentence in the passive voice:
The reforms could have affected a large number of marginal workers.

**Options:**
- (A) A large number of marginal workers were affected by the reforms.
- (B) A large number of marginal workers could have been affected by the reforms.
- (C) A large number of marginal workers could have affected the reforms.
- (D) A large number of marginal workers can be affected by the reforms.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:**
The active voice structure uses modal perfect "could have + past participle (affected)". In passive voice, the object "a large number of marginal workers" becomes the subject, followed by "could have been + past participle (affected)" and the agent "by the reforms".

### Question 16
- **Section**: Analytical & Logical Reasoning
- **Topic**: Number Series
- **Difficulty**: Medium

**Question:**
Each of the following questions has a series of numbers which follow a definite pattern except for one number. Identify the incorrect term of the series:
4, 18, 48, 100, 180, 295

**Options:**
- (A) 18
- (B) 295
- (C) 180
- (D) 48

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:**
The sequence follows the pattern $n^3 - n^2$:
- $2^3 - 2^2 = 8 - 4 = 4$
- $3^3 - 3^2 = 27 - 9 = 18$
- $4^3 - 4^2 = 64 - 16 = 48$
- $5^3 - 5^2 = 125 - 25 = 100$
- $6^3 - 6^2 = 216 - 36 = 180$
- $7^3 - 7^2 = 343 - 49 = 294$

Instead of $294$, the term given is $295$. Hence, 295 is incorrect.

### Question 19
- **Section**: Analytical & Logical Reasoning
- **Topic**: Syllogism
- **Difficulty**: Medium

**Question:**
**Statements:**
1) Some letters are pins.
2) Some keys are eyes.
3) All letters are keys.

**Conclusions:**
1) Some pins are not letters.
2) All pins are letters.

**Options:**
- (A) Only 1 follows
- (B) Only 2 follows
- (C) Either 1 or 2 follows
- (D) Both 1 and 2 follows

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:**
We are given that "Some letters are pins", which means there is a non-empty intersection between letters and pins. However, this does not conclusively tell us whether the set of pins is completely contained inside the set of letters, or if some pins lie outside letters.
Conclusion 1 ("Some pins are not letters") and Conclusion 2 ("All pins are letters") form a standard complementary pair (Some not / All). Therefore, either 1 or 2 follows.

### Question 22
- **Section**: Analytical & Logical Reasoning
- **Topic**: Linear Arrangement / Puzzles
- **Difficulty**: Medium

**Question:**
An organization decides to distribute seven types of laptops on weekdays starting from Monday to Sunday. The seven laptops to be distributed are Sony, Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, Lenovo, and HCL.
- Dell laptops are to be given on Saturday.
- Lenovo laptops are given just after Asus laptops.
- Asus laptops are not given on Wednesday.
- HP laptops are given three days after Asus laptops.
- Acer laptops are distributed just after Sony laptops.
- HCL laptops are distributed before Acer laptops.
- HP laptops are distributed after Dell laptops.

If HCL is related with Tuesday, Sony is related with Wednesday, similarly Lenovo is related with which day?

**Options:**
- (A) Thursday
- (B) Friday
- (C) Saturday
- (D) Sunday

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:**
From the given constraints:
1. Dell is Saturday.
2. HP is after Dell $\implies$ HP is Sunday.
3. HP is 3 days after Asus $\implies$ Asus is Thursday.
4. Lenovo is right after Asus $\implies$ Lenovo is Friday.
5. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday remain for HCL, Sony, Acer. Sony is immediately followed by Acer, and HCL is before Acer $\implies$ Monday = HCL, Tuesday = Sony, Wednesday = Acer.

The relationship mapped in the question shifts each entity forward by $+1$ day:
- HCL (Monday) $\rightarrow$ Tuesday
- Sony (Tuesday) $\rightarrow$ Wednesday
- Lenovo (Friday) $\rightarrow$ **Saturday**

### Question 28
- **Section**: Analytical & Logical Reasoning
- **Topic**: Clocks
- **Difficulty**: Medium

**Question:**
At what time, between 4 and 5 o'clock, will the minute and hour hands be together?

**Options:**
- (A) 24 and $\frac{5}{7}$ minutes past 4 o'clock.
- (B) 26 and $\frac{9}{11}$ minutes past 4 o'clock.
- (C) 21 and $\frac{9}{11}$ minutes past 4 o'clock.
- (D) 20 and $\frac{7}{9}$ minutes past 4 o'clock.

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:**
At 4 o'clock, the minute hand is at 0 minutes and the hour hand is at the 20-minute mark (angle $= 120^\circ$).
Relative speed of minute hand to hour hand $= 6^\circ - 0.5^\circ = 5.5^\circ/\text{minute} = \frac{11}{2}^\circ/\text{minute}$.
Time taken to cover $120^\circ$:
$$t = \frac{120}{\frac{11}{2}} = \frac{240}{11} = 21\frac{9}{11}\text{ minutes}$$
Thus, the hands are together at **21 and $\frac{9}{11}$ minutes past 4 o'clock**.

**Question:**
If 1st July, 1977 was a Friday, then 1st July 1970 was a _______?

**Options:**
- (A) Wednesday
- (B) Thursday
- (C) Sunday
- (D) Tuesday

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
Count odd days between July 1, 1970 and July 1, 1977 (7 years):
- Leap years in this period: 1972 and 1976 (2 leap years)
- Ordinary years: $7 - 2 = 5$ ordinary years
Total odd days:
$$\text{Odd days} = 5 \times 1 + 2 \times 2 = 9 \equiv 2 \pmod 7$$
Moving backward from Friday by 2 days: Friday $\rightarrow$ Thursday $\rightarrow$ **Wednesday**.

### Question 34

**Question:**
In a certain code language:

- 'your trip be customized' is written as 'ha ni ta de'

Which of the following represents 'good trip always interesting'?

**Options:**
- (A) ni fa de la
- (B) ka de la ni
- (C) fa ka ta de
- (D) ni fa la ka

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:**
Comparing sentences to decode each word:

2. 'customized' is common between statements 2 and 3 $\implies \text{customized} = \text{ta}$.
3. 'good' is common between statements 3 and 4 $\implies \text{good} = \text{ka}$.
4. In statement 3, remaining word 'always' $\implies \text{always} = \text{fa}$.
5. In statement 1 and 4, 'make' is common $\implies \text{make} = \text{pa}$.
6. In statement 1 and 2, 'your' is common $\implies \text{your} = \text{de}$.
7. From statement 1, the remaining word 'interesting' $\implies \text{interesting} = \text{la}$.

Thus, 'good trip always interesting' is coded as **ni fa la ka** (in any order).
